SUSHIL CHAPAGAIN / THE PERSON BEHIND THE IDEAS

I'm currently in the first year of a Bachelor's degree in Artificial Intelligence (B.Tech AI). My interest in AI sits alongside a broader curiosity about software engineering—how applications are designed, how systems fit together, and how an idea can become a useful digital product.

I'm at the beginning of my technical journey and am developing foundational knowledge and practical skills. I want to learn by understanding the fundamentals, experimenting thoughtfully, and improving with each thing I build.

What draws me to technology is its potential to make everyday tasks easier and help address real problems. I hope to keep learning, collaborate with others, and turn promising ideas into practical work over time.

Personal philosophy

Stay curious. Learn the why behind the how. Experiment with care. Build things that are useful to people.
